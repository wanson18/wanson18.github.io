Wanson Automation

Wanson Automation is a private, internal tool used by Wanson Enterprise and Wanson Enterprise (M) Sdn. Bhd., Menglembu, Perak, Malaysia, to automate stock records and reporting for its own cooking-oil business.

It is not offered to the public. Only the business owner's own Google accounts sign in to it.
